About Rabbit Bathware

EXCELLENCE IN CERAMIC SANITARY WARE CRAFTSMANSHIP

Our Heritage & Manufacturing Excellence

Based in Morbi, Gujarat — the ceramic capital of India — Rabbit Bathware is a premier manufacturer and exporter of high-grade vitreous china ceramic sanitary ware, designer art basins, luxury vanity sets, and granite quartz sinks.

Every piece is fired at 1250°C in advanced tunnel kilns, guaranteeing superior structural density, non-porous hygiene, and long-lasting glossy brilliance.
